ABOUT WPAY

WPay

Collections. Payouts. Clarity.

WPay is a business-focused payment solutions brand built to simplify the way businesses collect and manage payments. Our focus is on customer collections, business payouts and clear transaction visibility across a range of industries.

We serve the payment needs of e-commerce businesses, retail stores, education providers, and eligible forex, trading and gaming platforms. Our currency focus is Indian Rupees (INR) and Bangladeshi Taka (BDT), with additional currency support planned.

Our approach is simple: understand each business’s payment requirements and provide a more connected, organised payment experience as it grows.

Which currencies does WPay focus on?+

Indian Rupees (INR) and Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Additional currency support is planned; no other currency is presented as currently available.

Are the collection previews live?+

No. Both previews contain fictional sample amounts. They illustrate a possible collection overview and are not live accounts or guarantees of production features.

Are all payment methods available to my business?+

Methods and integrations are confirmed during onboarding. Logos identify payment-method examples; they do not imply a partnership, endorsement or guaranteed availability.

Which businesses can enquire?+

E-commerce, retail and education businesses, and eligible forex, trading and gaming businesses. Service availability depends on eligibility, onboarding and the markets served.

How do I ask about a refund or an existing service?+

For a purchase-related refund, contact the merchant you paid. Existing WPay business clients should use the channel in their service agreement. Read our Refund Policy for the website scope.
